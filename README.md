# IS 401 Semester Project

## App Summary:

The BYU gym at the Smith Fieldhouse can become crowded during peak hours, leaving students and faculty waiting to enter or use popular equipment. Those delays can stretch a short workout into a much longer visit or force people to cut it short. This app helps BYU gym users decide when to go by showing current occupancy compared with the gym’s capacity. It also tracks crowd levels over time so users can spot busier and quieter hours, days, and months. An equipment availability tracker and historical wait time estimates help users plan around busy areas and expected lines. Optional alerts notify users when crowd levels fall below a threshold they choose.
